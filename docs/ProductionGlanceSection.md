# ProductionGlanceSection

**Your production at a glance** band — sits under the hero’s blue ticker.

Stats sit in clay `.neu-pad` tiles with unique marks: bars (volume), house (loan size), hourglass (days). Extra vertical padding (`py-28 sm:py-36 lg:py-40`) gives the band room under the hero ticker.
